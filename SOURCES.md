# Release-order notes

# Core catalog release notes

Verified 18 November 2026. Dates refer to the earliest public release in any region, rather than North American release dates. The catalog includes distinct originals, remakes, remasters and meaningfully enhanced versions. Routine emulation, Virtual Console, Nintendo Switch Online, unchanged ports and compilation discs are not separate core entries. The two games bundled as A Link to the Past & Four Swords for Game Boy Advance are separate guide entries with the same release date.

- [Nintendo Japanese series history](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/character/zelda/history/index.html?lang=ja) supplies the original Japanese launch dates, including the initial 1986 game, Zelda II, A Link to the Past, Link's Awakening, Ocarina of Time, Majora's Mask and Oracle games.
- [Nintendo US series history](https://www.nintendo.com/us/explore/characters/zelda/history/) supplies later launch dates and confirms the 2026 Ocarina of Time remake. Its original games' dates are generally US dates, so earlier Japanese or European dates are used where appropriate. For A Link to the Past & Four Swords, this official history says December 2, 2002; some secondary catalogs instead say December 3. This catalog follows Nintendo's December 2 date.
- [Wikipedia consolidated media catalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Legend_of_Zelda_media) cross-checks originals and enhanced releases, including Link's Awakening DX (1998-12-12), Ocarina of Time 3D (2011-06-16), Four Swords Anniversary Edition (2011-09-28), Majora's Mask 3D (2015-02-13) and Twilight Princess HD (2016-03-04).
- [Nintendo October 2013 IR release table](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2013/131031e.pdf) establishes The Wind Waker HD's September 20, 2013 North American digital launch. It precedes Japan's September 26 physical launch and the October 4 North American physical launch.
- [Nintendo August 2025 IR release table](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2025/250801_2e.pdf?pubDate=20250802) confirms June 5, 2025 for both Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom Nintendo Switch 2 Editions.
- [Nintendo Ocarina of Time launch announcement](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/its-time-experience-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-reborn-exclusively-for-nintendo-switch-2/) confirms November 5, 2026 and says the Switch 2 remake was built from the ground up. [Nintendo product page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-125697/) independently lists that date.

## Master Quest distribution date

The earliest public distribution date is retained as **November 28, 2002**. This refers to the Japanese playable bonus disc distributed during The Wind Waker preorder promotion, not the December 13 release of The Wind Waker itself.

- [Contemporaneous GAME Watch report, November 15, 2002](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/20021115/zelda.htm) says Nintendo's campaign giving preorder customers the disc starts November 28. It explicitly describes both included games as complete, playable from beginning to ending, rather than demos.
- [Contemporaneous Dengeki report, November 15, 2002](https://dengekionline.com/data/news/2002/11/15/d1639083c53b3eb9d9e72fb0295eedc2.html) also reports the November 28 preorder-disc campaign.
- [Zelda Wiki bonus-disc record](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda%3A_Ocarina_of_Time_/_Master_Quest) and [MobyGames release record](https://www.mobygames.com/game/21968/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-master-quest/) list November 28, 2002.
- [Nintendo's 3DS description of Master Quest](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/3ds/aqej/forall/) confirms the original GameCube release was a limited preorder bonus in 2002, though it does not provide the day.

Game titles sometimes include a platform suffix solely to distinguish same-named versions in the guide list. This is a display disambiguation, not an asserted subtitle on the game packaging. Twilight Princess and the original Breath of the Wild combine their launch platforms in one original-game entry.


# Expanded Zelda catalog: research notes

Checked against released games through November 18, 2026. Dates mean first release in any region; deliberately partial dates retain their actual uncertainty. Routine emulation reissues, cameos, unofficial fan games, and unreleased games are excluded.

## Early licensed and LCD releases

- [Media catalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Legend_of_Zelda_media): Zelda on Game & Watch (August 1989), Nelsonic Game Watch (October 5, 1989), Barcode Battler II adaptation (1992), and both 1993 CD-i releases (October 10, 1993). The 1992 title is the licensed Barcode Battler II adaptation of A Link to the Past, not a second SNES game.
- [CD-i release history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda_CD-i_games): Link: The Faces of Evil and Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon were simultaneous releases.
- [Zelda's Adventure specialist research](https://zelda-temple.net/zeldas-adventure/): first sold in the Netherlands in February 1996, then the UK on May 10, 1996. This article cites contemporary CD-i magazine records. Many older catalogs instead repeat 1994 or 1995, which correspond to previews and the final build. The dedicated [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zelda%27s_Adventure) now uses 1996, whereas the umbrella media catalog still carries 1994. Display 1996; the JSON records the earliest known month without inventing a day.

## Broadcast releases

- [BS Zelda dates](https://datacrystal.tcrf.net/wiki/BS_Zelda_no_Densetsu): MAP1 began August 6, 1995; MAP2 began December 30, 1995. These are distinct quest maps, with episodes grouped into their respective release rather than one row per weekly broadcast.
- [Ancient Stone Tablets](https://www.mobygames.com/game/55757/bs-zelda-no-densetsu-inishie-no-sekiban/): first broadcast March 30, 1997. Japanese title: BS Zelda no Densetsu: Inishie no Sekiban (BSゼルダの伝説 古代の石盤). The readable English title in the JSON is an established translation, not an official Western release title.

## Navi Trackers and Tingle games

- [Nintendo Four Swords Plus product page](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ngc/g4sj/index.html): March 18, 2004. [Nintendo development interview](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/wiiu/interview/bczj/vol1/index.html) confirms the Japanese package included Hyrule Adventure, Shadow Battle, and Navi Trackers. Navi Trackers is separately listed here because it is a distinct included game; it was not sold separately.
- [Nintendo Rupeeland page](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ds/achj/index.html): September 2, 2006, Nintendo DS.
- [Tingle's Balloon Fight DS](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Tingle%27s_Balloon_Fight): April 12, 2007, Nintendo DS; distributed as a Japanese Club Nintendo reward.
- [Media catalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Legend_of_Zelda_media): Link's Crossbow Training first released in North America on November 19, 2007, Wii (Japan's 2008 date is not the earliest).
- [Nintendo Dekisugi Tingle Pack page](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ds/dsiware/kcpj/index.html): June 24, 2009, DSiWare; primarily tools plus minigames.
- [Nintendo Balloon Trip page](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ds/bcnj/index.html): August 6, 2009. Irozuki Tingle no Koi no Balloon Trip is a Japan-only Nintendo DS game; Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love is an English translation of its title.

## Warriors and other modern spin-offs

- [Hyrule Warriors release history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrule_Warriors): August 14, 2014, Wii U, Japan.
- [Koei Tecmo Legends official page](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-3ds/): January 21, 2016 Japanese launch (Nintendo 3DS). This is the earliest release, preceding the March Western launch.
- [Contemporary My Nintendo launch report](https://www.inside-games.jp/article/2016/03/17/96960.html?pickup_list_click2=true): Picross available on March 17, 2016 in Japan; the Western March 31 launch is later.
- [Koei Tecmo Definitive Edition page](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda-sw/index.html): March 22, 2018 Japanese Nintendo Switch release; the Western May 18 release is later.
- [Nintendo Cadence page](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-download-software/Cadence-of-Hyrule-Crypt-of-the-NecroDancer-Featuring-The-Legend-of-Zelda-1533129.html): June 13, 2019, Nintendo Switch.
- [Nintendo Age of Calamity page](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-games/Hyrule-Warriors-Age-of-Calamity-1838129.html?nsuid=70010000034701): November 20, 2020, Nintendo Switch.
- [Nintendo Age of Imprisonment launch](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/hyrule-warriors-age-of-imprisonment-launches-today-exclusively-on-nintendo-switch-2/): November 6, 2025, Nintendo Switch 2.

## Collections: footnote entries, excluded from the JSON

- The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition — November 14, 2003 (PAL first release), Nintendo GameCube. Includes The Legend of Zelda, Zelda II: The Adventure of Link, Ocarina of Time, and Majora's Mask, plus a Wind Waker demo. [Collection listing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Legend_of_Zelda_media#Collections).
- Game & Watch: The Legend of Zelda — November 12, 2021. Includes The Legend of Zelda, Zelda II: The Adventure of Link, Link's Awakening, and a Link-themed version of Vermin. [Nintendo product page and date](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/hardware/gamewatch/zelda/index.html?cphard=switch). This is a different device from the 1989 LCD Zelda Game & Watch.
